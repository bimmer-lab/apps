## Nov 25 2026

- **Copiloto de voz 0.8.2:** the welcome was often missing: the head unit starts the Store's apps only once its own HMI is up, so the app often came up with the engine already running and took it for a start on the road. Now, when the head unit started less than 6 minutes ago (os.uptime), that first "engine running" still gives the welcome, late. A notice the car's audio could not say yet (just after start) is tried again after 6 s, up to 3 times, in the first 4 minutes. MainApp has no way to start one app before the others (mguAppStartType: manually, autostart or lum).
- **Copiloto de voz 0.8.1:** the car's catalog names the interface de.bmw.body.doorsandwindows.WindowsRoofMaster (0.8.0 asked for de.bmw.body.WindowsRoofMaster, which the build dropped as missing). Its instances are logged too.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.8.0:** the windows are the fields statusWindowsRoofMasterDRD and statusWindowsRoofMasterPSD of the interface WindowsRoofMaster (each with positionWindowRegulator, statusPositionWindowRegulator, statusDriveWindowRegulator and statusErrorWindowRegulator), found in the car's catalog. The app listens to them (de.bmw.body.WindowsRoofMaster, optional: a unit without it still installs the app) and learns from them together with the doors' signal; Diagnóstico shows each window's position live. The catalog's id for the interface is logged in case it differs.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.6:** the windows are in the wrappers StatusWindowsRoofMaster<door>Wrapper (positionWindowRegulator, statusPositionWindowRegulator, statusDriveWindowRegulator, statusErrorWindowRegulator) and PositionWindowRegulator<door>Wrapper. The app now logs the attribute (and its service) whose type is one of those wrappers, exactly.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.5:** the car's catalog has the windows as statusPositionWindowRegulator<door> and statusDriveWindowRegulator<door> (stopped / opening / closing), not in CentralLockingSystemAndPanelStatus (its members are the doors, bonnet, boot lid and locking). When learning starts the app walks the catalog as JSON and logs which struct holds those fields and which attribute of which service carries it, so the next version can listen to it.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.4:** the car answered "Unknown method name get:CentralLockingSystemAndPanelStatus" (the field was declared to be listened to only): now also read. The catalog has a window regulator status type (DoorsAndWindowsTypes.StatusDriveWindowRegulator): when learning starts the app logs where the catalog uses it and the attributes of its own DoorsWindowsRoofMirrors config, to find the attribute that carries the windows. The Registro page leaves out the kit's routine lines (frame rates, screen on / off).
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.3:** still nothing learned in the car. When a learning ends with nothing, the app searches the unit's SOME/IP catalog (/etc/oap/someip/someip-interfaces.json) for names about windows and logs them with their service, to find where this car says its windows.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.2:** in the car «Aprender ventanillas» learned nothing. Each press now reads DoorsWindowsRoofMirrors afresh (a car may not send it again when only a window moves) and logs every field it holds, to see where this car puts its windows. Two steps only, the owner's car has two windows and no roof.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.1:** «Aprender ventanillas»: no public app reads the windows, so the app learns from the car which fields of DoorsWindowsRoofMirrors say "open": everything closed, then each window and the roof opened in turn (a press with nothing changed skips it); kept in the data folder, open while a learned field is off its closed value (flags or positions alike), the doors' switches never taken. Diagnóstico shows the windows live; the windows notice at switch-off uses what was learned (windows.js).
- **Fuel Link 0.4.0:** no tile in the apps menu and no page any more, as the owner asked: it only serves the fuel and range to the phone's page. It starts with the car and runs in background() only; nothing is ever drawn. The kit's build takes app.json "hidden": true (no entry point, as BMW's own start widgets have none).
- **Copiloto de voz 0.7.0:** the voice now sounds in the car. As the owner asked: the battery notice is gone (with its diagnostic read, tile, setting and row: ten notices); one voice only, Spanish Elvira (the voices tool **copiloto-voz-voces** 1.0.1 is 13 files, 1 MB, now in tools-prebuilt/, no longer inside the app's folder, so the Store fetches 0.9 MB to build the app); no language or voice settings. The demo leaves 1.5 s between two phrases, and a phrase's end is taken only once the player has played it (the last item's time, sent right after playFile, ended the next phrase at once). The page opens at once: background() returns before subscribing to the signals, redraws are coalesced (at most every 0.4 s, else every 30 s), the recordings are looked up once.
- **Each app on its own in the Store:** Cluster Themes, Fuel Link and Copiloto de voz are listed one by one in mgu-repo.json with build.shared (their folder, the kit and their own shared/ files). Before, the "apps/*" rule had no shared list, so the Store took the whole repository as each app's content and rebuilt all three after any change anywhere; now a change in one app's folder updates only that app. The kit's build no longer has anything only for Copiloto de voz.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.6.2 (diagnostics):** in the car the player now plays each phrase to its end, yet nothing is heard and the volume knob does nothing meanwhile. The app logs what the Audio Manager says 1 s into each phrase and after it: the connections with their state, the main sink's volume and mute, and the media source.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.6.2:** the car played the phrases to their end but nothing was heard. Connect answers at once while the Audio Manager brings the speakers up after it, so a 2 s phrase could be over before; the app now waits for the connection to be CS_CONNECTED (at most 3 s) and 0.7 s more before playing. - **Copiloto de voz 0.6.1:** in the car the phrases now play from /var/opt ("end of the phrase"). - **Copiloto de voz 0.6.0:** the recordings are now their own Store tool, **copiloto-voz-voces** 1.0.0, installed with the app in /var/opt/copiloto-voz-voces/current (root's, 0755 / 0644), where BMW's media engine can open them, as RideTube's audio under /var/opt; the app's own folders, opened for passing through, and /tmp stay as fallbacks. - **Updated from the original repositories (Nov 25):** Cluster Themes 0.20.2 (bimmer-lab's 0.19.10 together with 0.20.1's lighter tick: the Waze map shown inside the theme again with Waze on Cluster 0.14.5+ and cluster-render 0.8.2+, now also in M view and Vector; with this repository's per-drive-mode map switches kept), Redline 2.0.51 (still without the HUD), Ambient config 2.7.26, Maps on cluster 0.6.16, cluster-render 0.8.13, Waze on Cluster 0.14.15. ## Nov 24 2026

- **Lighter on the head unit, so CarPlay keeps its connection.** CarPlay, the HMI and these apps share the same CPU and memory; when an app takes too much of them, wireless CarPlay drops. Not yet tested on a car.
  - **Every app runs below the car's own processes** (`kit/runtime/priority.js`): the lowest I/O class and a lower CPU priority (nice 10; Fuel Link 19), for every thread. Nothing changes while the unit has CPU to spare; when it is busy, CarPlay and the HMI go first. In the kit of this repository and in the vendored kits (Redline, Drag Timer, Dyno, Ambient config, DPF Monitor, iDrive Monitor, RideTube); `scripts/vendor_sync.py` applies it again after each update. `config.nice` in an app's `app.json` changes it (0 leaves the app as it was). Maps on cluster runs natively and is not affected.
  - **Fuel Link 0.3.0:** the page was drawn again (1600 x 520, in JavaScript, then a PNG) at every fuel signal, many times a minute while driving, even with the page closed. Now it is drawn only while it is on screen, at most once every 5 s, and a reading equal to the last one only updates its time. The reading is saved only when it changed. The tailnet address is looked for every 5 min once found (before: every 30 s, with a redraw each time). Idle connections from the phone are closed after 2 s.
  - **Cluster Themes 0.20.1:** the 20 Hz tick slows down when nothing would change: 4 Hz while nothing of ours is on the cluster (Off, BMW's own in this drive mode, renderer not up yet), 10 Hz standing with the engine off. Driving, idling or with the shift light it stays at 20 Hz, so the needles and the 0-60 timer are as before.
- **Copiloto de voz 0.5.1:** the voices: 153 recordings (14 phrases × 11 voices, Spanish, Chinese and English), made with scripts/generar_voces.py. Ryan's "good morning" did not come out; Thomas's is said in its place.

A demo: a simulated trip in which all 11 notices happen, said through the real queue and speaker (parked only). Two new pages: Diagnóstico (each signal, the voice and each notice's result, green/grey/red with the reason) and Registro (the log's last lines on the screen). A phrase editor page (tools/editor_frases.html). Not yet tested on a car.
